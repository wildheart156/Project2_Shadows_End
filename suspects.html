Suspect Profiles

Everyone at the dinner had a reason to want the benefactor dead. Examine each suspect and look for inconsistencies in their stories.

Jonathan - Family Lawyer

Age: 32

Relationship: Albert Jenkins' family lawyer.

Investigate Suspect

Jonathan handled Albert's legal and financial affairs.

Possible Motive: Albert discovered suspicious financial activity.

Evidence: Financial records show suspicious transactions involving Albert's accounts.

Dorothy Moore - Business Partner

Age: 38

Relationship: Albert Jenkins' business partner.

Investigate Suspect

Dorothy helped Albert build his company, but Albert planned to remove her from an important business deal.

Possible Motive: Revenge for being betrayed.

Evidence: A threatening letter from Dorothy was discovered in Albert's bedroom.

Anna Whitlow - Personal Assistant

Age: 30

Relationship: Albert Jenkins' personal assistant.

Investigate Suspect

Anna worked for Albert for three years. She recently discovered that he planned to fire her and blame her for missing company money and documents.

Possible Motive: Revenge and protecting her reputation.

Evidence: Security footage shows Anna entering Albert's private movie theater before dinner. A bloody knife was also discovered underneath her bed.

Bentley Preston - Butler

Age: 60

Relationship: Albert Jenkins' butler.

Investigate Suspect

Bentley worked for Albert's family for over 10 years. Albert recently planned to sell the mansion, leaving Bentley without a job.

Possible Motive: Anger over losing his job.

Evidence: Bentley was seen near the movie theater shortly before the power outage.

Agnes Jenkins - Mother

Age: 72

Relationship: Albert Jenkins' mother.

Investigate Suspect

Agnes helped Albert build the family business. Their relationship became worse when Albert secretly sold family properties without telling her.

Possible Motive: Anger over Albert betraying the family and taking control of their wealth.

Evidence: A handwritten warning letter from Agnes was discovered inside Albert's private movie theater.
